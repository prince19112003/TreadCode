Edition & License Activation

TreadCode runs in Free Community Edition by default (Python visualizer unlocked). To unlock all courses (C, C++, Java, DSA, Web, and SmartBoard):

• Offline Activation (Air-Gapped / No Internet): Simply replace the default license.json file in this folder with the authorized license.json provided by your administrator. TreadCode will auto-activate on startup with 0% network required.
• In-App Online Activation: Open TreadCode → Settings → License → Enter your issued key.
• Purchase or Request License: To get an institutional offline license file for your school or lab, contact your administrator or email sales at treadcode.com.
